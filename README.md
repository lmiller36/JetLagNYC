# NYC Scavenger Hunt

A mobile-optimized website for an NYC scavenger hunt game where teams explore the five boroughs and complete challenges to earn points.

## 🎮 Live Site

Visit the game at: **[https://lmiller36.github.io/JetLagNYC](https://lmiller36.github.io/JetLagNYC)**

## 📱 Features

- **Challenges**: Complete tasks across different difficulty levels to earn points
- **Neighborhoods**: Explore NYC boroughs with varying point values based on distance from Midtown
- **Photo Submission**: Upload evidence to prove challenge completion
- **Bonus Points**: Compete for awards in categories like funniest or hardest challenges
- **Mobile-First Design**: Optimized for on-the-go gameplay

## 🏆 How to Play

1. Form a team
2. Browse available challenges and plan your route
3. Complete challenges across NYC
4. Take photos as proof
5. Submit your photos before the deadline
6. Compete for bonus category awards (🥇🥈🥉)
