Photo Submission Guidelines

📸 Photo Requirements

  • Photos must be clear and well-lit
  • Include yourself or team member in the photo
  • Show the challenge location or activity clearly
  • Take photos in landscape orientation when possible
  • Ensure photos are not blurry or too dark

📝 File Naming

  • Name files: "TeamName_ChallengeName_Location"
  • Example: "TeamAwesome_EatPizza_LittleItaly"
  • Use underscores instead of spaces
  • Keep file names under 50 characters
  • Include timestamp if multiple attempts

📋 What to Include

  • Team name in the file name
  • Challenge being completed
  • Neighborhood or specific location
  • Timestamp (camera metadata is fine)
  • Any bonus criteria being attempted

⏰ Submission Timing

  • Submit photos as soon as possible after completion
  • Photos must be taken during the game period
  • Late submissions may not be counted
  • Check with organizers for deadline extensions
  • Batch upload is acceptable at end of day

✅ Valid Evidence

  • Photo clearly shows challenge completion
  • Team member visible in the photo
  • Location is identifiable
  • Photo taken during game timeframe
  • Meets specific challenge requirements

🚫 Invalid Submissions

  • Blurry or unclear photos
  • Photos without team members
  • Screenshots or downloaded images
  • Photos taken before/after game period
  • Staged or fake challenge completions
