🎯 Game Overview

The NYC Scavenger Hunt is a competitive game inspired by "Jet Lag the Game" where teams race across New York City to complete challenges and earn points. Teams must strategically choose which neighborhoods to visit and which challenges to complete to maximize their score.

Basic Concept

  • Teams compete across Manhattan, Queens, and Brooklyn
  • Complete challenges in different neighborhoods to earn points
  • Submit photo evidence of challenge completion
  • Strategic planning is key - some areas offer more points but are harder to reach
  • Game has a set time limit (determined by organizers)

🏆 Scoring System

Your total score is calculated by combining neighborhood points with challenge points. Understanding this system is crucial for strategic gameplay.

Neighborhood Points

Each neighborhood has a base point value. Areas farther from Midtown Manhattan offer higher base points.

⚠️ Important Rules:
  • Neighborhood points only count the FIRST time you complete a challenge there
  • You MUST complete a challenge in that location to earn the bonus

Challenge Points

Varies

Each challenge has its own point value based on difficulty. Some challenges offer bonus points for extra criteria.

📊 Scoring Example

Scenario: Complete a "Take a selfie with street art" challenge (5 points) in Williamsburg, Brooklyn (base: 8 points)

Calculation:

  • Neighborhood points: 8 points
  • Challenge points: 5 points
  • Total: 13 points
💡 Strategy Tip: Higher-point neighborhoods are typically farther from Midtown Manhattan, so plan your route carefully to balance travel time with point potential.

🎮 Challenge Categories

Challenges are organized into two categories.

General

Can be completed anywhere in NYC. Usually easier and worth fewer points.

Location-Specific

Must be completed in specific neighborhoods or at particular landmarks.

📸 Valid Photo Evidence

Photo evidence is crucial for scoring. Follow these rules to ensure your submissions are accepted.

✅ Required Elements

  • At least one team member must be visible in the photo
  • The challenge activity or location must be clearly shown
  • Photo must be taken during the official game timeframe
  • Image must be clear, well-lit, and not blurry
  • Location must be identifiable (street signs, landmarks, etc.)

❌ Invalid Evidence

  • Screenshots or downloaded images from the internet
  • Photos without any team members visible
  • Blurry, dark, or unclear images
  • Photos taken before or after the game period
  • Staged or fake challenge completions
  • Photos that don't clearly show the challenge being completed
📷 Good Photo Example

Challenge: "Eat a slice of pizza from a local pizzeria"

Good photo shows: Team member holding/eating pizza slice, pizzeria name visible in background, clear image taken during game time.

⭐ Bonus Points System

Many challenges offer bonus points for going above and beyond the basic requirements.

How Bonus Points Work

  • Bonus criteria are listed with each eligible challenge
  • You must complete the base challenge AND the bonus requirement
  • Bonus points are added to the base challenge points
  • Multiple bonus criteria can sometimes be combined
  • Bonus attempts must be clearly documented in photos
🌟 Bonus Example

Base Challenge: "Take a photo with a street performer" (10 points)

Bonus: "Give the performer a tip" (+5 points)

Total if bonus completed: 15 points + neighborhood points

⚖️ Gameplay Rules

Team Rules

  • Teams will be formed the day of the event
  • All team members must stay together during challenges
  • Teams cannot split up to complete multiple challenges simultaneously
  • Each team gets one submission per challenge

Transportation & Movement

  • Use any form of transportation (subway, bus, taxi, walking, etc.)
  • Follow all traffic laws and safety regulations
  • Be courteous to NYC residents and businesses

Challenge Completion

  • Each challenge can only be completed once per team
  • Challenges must be completed in the order you choose
  • No advance preparation or pre-staging allowed
  • All activities must be legal and safe

Submission Rules

  • Submit photos as soon as possible after completion
  • Use proper file naming conventions (see submission page)
  • Late submissions may not be counted
  • Organizers have final say on photo validity

🥇 How to Win

Victory Conditions

  • Team with the highest total points at the end of the game period wins
  • Points are calculated from all valid photo submissions
  • Ties are broken by the team with more completed challenges
  • If still tied, the team with more bonus points wins

🎯 Winning Strategies

  • Balance high-point neighborhoods with travel time
  • Focus on challenges you can complete efficiently
  • Plan your route to minimize backtracking
  • Consider bonus points for challenges you're already doing
  • Keep track of time - don't get stuck in one area too long
  • Have backup challenges ready in case your first choice isn't possible
🏆 Remember: The goal is to have fun exploring NYC while being competitive. Play fair, be safe, and enjoy the adventure!
